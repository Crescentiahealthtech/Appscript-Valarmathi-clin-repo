Record the s.7 basis for every patient
Care and billing are legitimate uses under s.7 — the patient came here to be treated, and neither is gated behind a tick box. What the register is missing for the patients already on file is not permission but the record that the basis was considered for them. This writes those two, for every patient with no row.

It deliberately does not touch the four real consents. A row saying a patient agreed to something nobody asked them is a false record, and a false consent record is worse than the gap it closes — it converts "we had not got round to asking" into "we claimed they agreed", and the clinic then sends their reports over WhatsApp on the strength of it.
